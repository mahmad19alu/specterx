About SpecterX

SpecterX is a creator workflow automation tool designed to streamline video editing, rendering, and publishing pipelines. It enables verified content creators to connect their social accounts, including TikTok, to securely schedule and publish their original video content directly via official APIs.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SpecterX respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data when you connect your accounts:

  • Data Access: SpecterX only requests account permissions strictly required to upload videos and read minimal profile status (such as username and account ID) via official OAuth authorization.
  • Data Usage: We do not sell, distribute, or monetize your personal account information. All authentication tokens are encrypted and used solely for scheduled video publishing operations initiated by you.
  • Data Deletion: You can revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings. If access is revoked, stored authorization tokens are permanently cleared.
